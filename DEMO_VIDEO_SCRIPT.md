# 2-Minute Demonstration Video Script: ResQ Lanka

> **Time Budget:** Exactly 120 seconds (2 minutes).  
> **Objective:** Hit all rubric points (Problem, Solution, Live Working Features, Deployed App, Expected Impact).

---

### [0:00 – 0:25] 1. Team Introduction & The Sri Lankan Problem
- **Speaker 1:**
  > "Hello everyone! We are Team [Group ID/Name], presenting **ResQ Lanka**, our digital solution for Sri Lanka's recurrent monsoon flood crisis.
  > Every year during the Southwest and Inter-monsoons, rivers like the Kelani, Kalu, and Gin Ganga overflow, submerging low-lying communities in Kolonnawa, Wellampitiya, Biyagama, and Ratnapura.
  > Flood victims face three critical bottlenecks: hydrological alerts are trapped in static PDF notices, official hotlines become jammed, and rescue boat teams cannot distinguish between urgent rooftop rescues and simple supply deliveries."

---

### [0:25 – 0:50] 2. The Solution & River Gauges Feature
- **Speaker 2:**
  > "To solve this, we built **ResQ Lanka** using **React, FastAPI, and MongoDB**.
  > On our live dashboard, responders and residents can immediately see real-time flood warning stages.
  > In our **River Gauges** tab, users can search and filter by river basin or alert stage. For example, checking Nagalagam Street immediately shows a 7.20-meter Major Flood level with high-risk warning tags for Kolonnawa and Wellampitiya."

---

### [0:50 – 1:20] 3. Emergency SOS Form & AI Triage Dispatch
- **Speaker 3:**
  > "When a resident is in danger, they use our **Request Evacuation Form**. Notice our input validation: entering an invalid phone number or invalid headcount immediately prompts a friendly error message enforcing Sri Lankan standards.
  > When we submit a distress call for a family stranded on a rooftop with an infant and elderly grandmother, our **AI Emergency Triage Engine** immediately analyzes the depth, vulnerability factors, and text.
  > It assigns a 98/100 Critical score and recommends dispatching a Sri Lanka Navy rescue boat. The alert instantly appears on our live **SOS Dispatch Board**, where volunteer boat crews can toggle statuses from *Pending* to *Dispatched* to *Rescued*."

---

### [1:20 – 1:45] 4. Relief Shelter Directory & Processing
- **Speaker 4:**
  > "For displaced families, our **Relief Shelter Directory** displays designated temples and schools with dynamic capacity calculators and available bed meters.
  > Responders can filter by district and see specific requested donations—such as infant milk powder or dry rations—and directly call camp coordinators."

---

### [1:45 – 2:00] 5. Deployment & Sri Lankan Impact
- **Speaker 1 / All:**
  > "Our application is live and publicly accessible on the web, fully responsive on both mobile phones and desktop command centers.
  > ResQ Lanka eliminates life-threatening delays and ensures every rescue boat is dispatched where it is needed most.
  > Thank you!"
